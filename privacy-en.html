Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Season Islands game for iOS and Android and this support site.

Information handled during gameplay

Season Islands is a puzzle game that does not require an account or sign-in. The game contains no advertising, advertising ID use, tracking, analytics service, or third-party crash-reporting service. During gameplay the app does not collect or send your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or device identifiers to the developer. Game assets and music run on your device; music is not streamed from the internet.

Game records stored on your device

Language, difficulty and audio preferences, completed/unlocked islands, best move counts, and your current attempt are stored on your device so you can continue playing. The game does not upload these records to a server. Your operating system's own backup and device-transfer features may apply separately, depending on your system and account settings.

To remove local records, delete the app together with its data. Archiving or offloading the app may preserve data. Manage any operating-system backup copies through the relevant account's backup settings. The developer cannot remotely access your local game records.

Optional support correspondence

The contact link opens your chosen email application. You decide whether to send a message. The game does not automatically send messages, save files, identifiers, or diagnostic attachments. If you contact us, your email address, display name and any message or attachments you choose to share are handled to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Support correspondence is not used for advertising, marketing or profiling and is not sold.

Email services used to deliver and store messages also process them under their own terms. Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve your request and provide necessary follow-up; applicable legal obligations may require longer retention. You may request deletion of support correspondence that is no longer needed through Settings → About → Support in the game. This applies to records under the developer's control; you may need to manage copies and backups held by your own email provider separately.

External links and platform services

Artist, music-source, license and support links open the relevant browser or application only when you choose them. Season Islands does not attach player information or game records to these links. Spotify, Pixabay, your email provider and other services you visit have their own privacy policies. Apple and Google's store, operating-system, purchase, backup and optional diagnostic services are also governed by the respective platform's terms; they are not integrated into the game as advertising or analytics SDKs.

Support website

We do not use accounts, contact forms, cookies, or visitor analytics on this site. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement for details.

Security, your requests and changes

Local records are stored in the operating system's app storage area. Support correspondence is accessed for support purposes. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal or sensitive information. You can request access, correction or deletion and ask privacy questions through Settings → About → Support in the game. We do not knowingly request children's personal information in the game; contact us if you believe it was included in a support message.

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated. The current update date appears at the top of this page.
